# Image direction

All photographs and the crema texture were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. No reference image was used.

## Origin — assets/origin.jpg

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed landscape photography for a cinematic luxury coffee roastery website. Create a panoramic editorial photograph of dramatic Ethiopian highlands at dawn, deep mist-filled valleys and layered mountain ridgelines, dense forest and tiny coffee terraces in the foreground, diffuse pale ivory sky occupying the top quarter. Analog medium-format photography, atmospheric black and white with slightly warm silver shadows, restrained fine grain, sharp organic detail in foreground fading into distant mist. Exceptional quiet, monumental, sophisticated composition, like a luxury travel magazine. No people, no text, no watermark. Wide 3:2 landscape.

## Coffee house — assets/cafe.jpg

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: hospitality editorial photography for fictional specialty coffee brand NOIR BEAN. Create a beautiful wide architectural photograph of an intimate brutalist specialty coffee house, large brushed stainless steel espresso machine on a long dark walnut bar, one sculptural stone stool in foreground, warm plaster walls, tall windows with slatted light, shadowy rear room, fresh espresso in a tiny white ceramic cup. Modern Kyoto meets Scandinavian concrete architecture, extremely tasteful and restrained. Late afternoon cinematic warm sunlight, deep charcoal shadows, chestnut timber and silver details, subtle film grain, real material texture, analog design magazine photography. No people, no readable signage or text, no logos, no watermark. Wide 3:2 landscape.

The three coffee packages and 3D beans are original code-generated assets, editable in the source files. Café strips use different crops of the same generated space.

## Ritual poster — assets/ritual-poster.jpg

Original generated luxury coffee advertisement in a near-black studio. A warm ivory, gently flared ceramic cup floats on the right at a three-quarter angle. A dark glossy espresso arc collides with a silky ivory cream ribbon, with suspended droplets and marbled crema. A large warm ivory disc glows behind the cup. Fine steam, left-side negative space, long studio reflections, tactile ceramic glaze, realistic liquid thickness. Restrained warm neutral palette. No typography, logos, watermark, or cartoon treatment. Used for the reduced-motion and WebGL fallback composition.

## Crema surface — assets/ritual-surface.jpg

Original generated square macro texture, flat overhead view filled entirely with coffee. Deep nearly black espresso, rich amber crema, and ivory milk form an organic clockwise spiral with fine tendrils and microfoam bubbles. High-speed commercial beverage photography, broad dark regions between ribbons, diffuse neutral studio light, dark brown edges. No cup, other objects, typography, or watermark. Used as a compressed texture with scroll-driven shader distortion.
